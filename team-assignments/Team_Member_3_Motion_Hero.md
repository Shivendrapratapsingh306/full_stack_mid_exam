# Team Member 3: Motion & Animation Engineer

## Role Overview
You are the creative developer. Your job is to bring the website to life using animations, scroll effects, and custom cinematic sequences. 

## Core Tasks
1. **Cinematic Loader**: Build and tweak the spectacular `CinematicLoader.tsx` that splits the screen in half.
2. **Scroll Animations**: Manage the `Reveal.tsx` wrapper component that makes elements slide up gracefully.
3. **Hero & Character Animation**: Manage `AngaarHero.tsx` and implement the complex GSAP/scroll-tied character walking sequence (`CharacterJourney.tsx`).

## Key Files to Commit
- `src/components/ui/CinematicLoader.tsx`
- `src/components/ui/Reveal.tsx`
- `src/components/sections/AngaarHero.tsx`
- `src/components/sections/CharacterJourney.tsx`
