# Team Member 4: Frontend Layout Developer

## Role Overview
You are responsible for assembling the core body of the Landing Page. You take the reusable UI components built by Team Member 2 and combine them into responsive sections.

## Core Tasks
1. **Homepage Assembly**: Map out and import all the sections into `src/app/page.tsx`.
2. **Text & Video Sections**: Manage the `AboutSection.tsx` (incorporating the background video).
3. **Complex Layouts**: Build and refine the auto-sliding `IndustriesSection.tsx` carousel and the `ServicesPreview.tsx` grid with hover effects.
4. **Content Sections**: Manage `StatsSection.tsx`, `ProcessSection.tsx`, and `TestimonialSection.tsx`.

- `src/app/page.tsx`
- `src/components/sections/AboutSection.tsx`
- `src/components/sections/IndustriesSection.tsx`
